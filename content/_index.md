## The work

I work somewhere on the border between engineer and scientist. Depending on the
problem, that can mean writing code, designing an experiment, or trying to
understand how people work together.

I’ve helped build legal AI at Thomson Reuters, led data and machine learning
organizations and teams at Brightcove, Grainger, Verizon, and Capital One, and
worked on Watson Conversation and Chef Watson at IBM. Yes, there was a food
truck involved.

Before that, I did a Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon studying how people and companies
build open source software together. The connection between people and software
has been a recurring theme ever since. It's something that I never get tired of.
